MobilePay (Vipps) Payment Provider

Seamless payments for the Danish market

Integrate MobilePay Online (Vipps ePayment API v3) directly into your Odoo eCommerce and Invoicing flows. Enable your customers to pay effortlessly using their mobile phones.

Key Features

Secure & Compliant

Full support for Authorize & Capture flows, encrypted credentials, and HMAC-verified webhooks.

Enhanced UX

Phone number pre-fill and automatic E.164 formatting ensures a frictionless checkout experience for your customers.

Real-time Status

Immediate payment status updates via Webhooks and active polling fallback.

Full Refund Support

Process full or partial refunds directly from the Odoo backend.

Enterprise Ready

Fully compatible with Odoo Enterprise and Community editions. Supports Danish localization.
